# daveai-proof-ledger

**Use when:** after any run, test, repair, or verification step that produces evidence

## Steps
- Identify the gate or claim being evidenced.
- Collect artifacts: hashes, screenshots, command output, URLs, or smoke-test results.
- Write a concise ledger entry with timestamp, actor, and verdict.
- Cross-reference the entry in PROJECT_STATE.md or RUN_REPORT.md.

## Proof required
PROOF_LEDGER.md entry with artifact hashes and verdict
